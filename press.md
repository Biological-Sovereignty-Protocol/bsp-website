# Press & Media

Materials for journalists, researchers, and partners covering BSP.

## Boilerplate

> The Biological Sovereignty Protocol (BSP) is an open cryptographic standard that gives individuals permanent ownership of their biological data — genomics, clinical records, wearables, microbiome, and more. Built on Aptos and Arweave, BSP enables labs, hospitals, wearable devices, and AI systems to exchange health data under a universal, user-controlled consent model. BSP is MIT-licensed and stewarded by the Ambrósio Institute.

**Short version (280 chars):**

> BSP is an open protocol that gives every human cryptographic ownership of their biological data. Built on Aptos + Arweave. MIT-licensed. 210+ biomarkers. Stewarded by the Ambrósio Institute.
